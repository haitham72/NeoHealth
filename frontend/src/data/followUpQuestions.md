# Follow-up question bank

Starter set for the "Continue exploring" suggestions shown under each answer
(`lib/followUpQuestions.ts` reads this file). This replaces a larger question log that
was lost — fill it back up freely, one question per bullet.

Optional `[TAG]` prefix (`DHA` / `DoH` / `MOHAP`) lets the picker favor questions from the
same authority as the answer just given. Untagged questions are treated as general and
can surface for any answer.

- [DHA] What are the requirements for licensing a healthcare professional in Dubai?
- [DHA] What happens if a healthcare professional's license application is rejected?
- [DHA] How long is a DHA professional license valid before renewal is required?
- [DHA] What documentation is needed to renew a DHA facility license?
- [DoH] What is the structure of Abu Dhabi health regulations?
- [DoH] What are the telehealth licensing requirements in Abu Dhabi?
- [DoH] How does Abu Dhabi regulate patient data privacy for healthcare facilities?
- [MOHAP] Which emirates does MOHAP directly regulate as the federal health authority?
- [MOHAP] What are the federal requirements for opening a new pharmacy?
- [MOHAP] How does MOHAP handle licensing for healthcare professionals in the Northern Emirates?
- What counts as a superseded regulation, and why does it get excluded by default?
- How is the effective date of a regulation determined when a document has multiple dates printed on it?
- What's the difference between an official regulation and a research document in this system?
- How does the system decide when to abstain instead of answering?
- What does a "medium confidence" answer mean, and how is that different from a "low confidence" one?
- Can a regulation from one authority ever apply across jurisdictions?
- What's required to keep a healthcare facility's accreditation in force?
- How are disciplinary actions against a licensed professional documented in regulation?
